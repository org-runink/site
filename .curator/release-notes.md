# Release notes

## 20261122-143306 (8167cd5)

This release updates the Tide product documentation to reflect a shift from per-action approval to policy-based execution, and expands the Luna privacy policy with details on service metrics and food-related features.

### Added
*   **Luna Privacy Policy:** Added sections describing "Food orders and reservations" (noted as occurring only when asked) and "Service counts" (hourly, anonymous, no identifier).
*   **Tide Whitepaper:** Added a new section, "It acts within the limits you set, and shows its work," detailing automated execution under administrative policies, guardrails, and tamper-evident logging.

### Changed
*   **Tide Product Description:** Replaced "asks before it acts" messaging with "acts within the limits you set," emphasizing execution within administrator-defined boundaries rather than individual approval for every step.
*   **Tide Whitepaper:** Replaced the previous description of human approval for all actions with the new policy-based execution model, reserving specific high-risk actions for human oversight.
*   **Luna Privacy Policy:** Updated the "No analytics" bullet to clarify that the only usage metrics collected are anonymous hourly assistant run counts.
