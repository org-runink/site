# Release notes

## 20261111-142027 (cffec37)

## Release Notes

This update introduces dedicated privacy policies and account deletion flows for all Runink products (FACE, PULSE, TIDE, Luna), updates pricing pages to reflect the 2026-11-06 price book, and refreshes product and homepage content to align with current application features.

### Added
*   **Privacy & Compliance:**
    *   New privacy policies for FACE, PULSE, TIDE, and Luna, including details on publisher identity, response times, complaint procedures, and governing law.
    *   Account deletion pages and sections for all applications, adhering to each app's specific terms.
    *   Link to the TIDE privacy policy from the general privacy policy.
*   **Pricing:**
    *   Updated pricing pages for TIDE and PULSE reflecting the 2026-11-06 price book, available in four languages, including costs for automated voice agents.
    *   New pricing estimator on the FACE page detailing costs for FACE Lite ($85/$99), Dedicated ($165), services ($200/hour), and a $150 provisioning cap for approved orders.
*   **Content & Branding:**
    *   Product personas for FACE, PULSE, and TIDE (surfing pup) displayed on their respective product pages.
    *   Three new blog answer pages covering revenue leakage, freight claims, and cold-chain claims.
    *   Content updates for PULSE highlighting the one-line audit, daily LinkedIn draft, and Market sources.

### Changed
*   **Product Pages:**
    *   FACE product pages rebuilt to document current features without demo or mock claims; tour updated to match the current app.
    *   PULSE pages updated to follow Release 10; Android APK downloads removed.
    *   TIDE and general product pages updated to reflect the current TIDE console, River's desktop, and documentation.
    *   Homepage rebuilt to focus on one product and one first step, with content tailored to field operations and revenue leaders.
*   **Site Structure:**
    *   Footer standardized to a single five-column layout with functional language links.
    *   Public contact address updated to `contact@runink.org`.

### Fixed
*   **Privacy:**
    *   FACE privacy policy updated to match the application state after face#1955.
*   **UI/UX:**
    *   Full-width row labels on mobile devices for the homepage.
    *   Underline added to the comparison note link on the homepage.
    *   Removed an internal source citation from a public industries page.
