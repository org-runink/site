## 20261108-132312 (3ba526c)

## Release Notes

This update introduces new privacy policies and pricing details for TIDE, PULSE, and FACE, updates product pages to reflect current features and personas, and refines site content and navigation.

### Added
*   **Privacy Policies:**
    *   Added a dedicated TIDE privacy policy at `/tide-privacy/`.
    *   Added privacy policies for FACE and PULSE, including specific account-deletion sections.
    *   Updated FACE, PULSE, and Luna policies to explicitly name the publisher, response times, complaint contacts, and governing law.
*   **Pricing:**
    *   Added TIDE and PULSE pricing based on the 2026-11-06 price book, available in four languages, including costs for automated voice agents.
    *   Added a pricing estimator to the FACE page (FACE Lite: $85/$99, Dedicated: $165; services: $200/hour; $150 provisioning cap on approved orders).
*   **Content & Branding:**
    *   Added product personas to FACE, PULSE, and TIDE product pages (including the "surfing pup" for TIDE).
    *   Added three blog answer pages covering revenue leakage, freight claims, and cold-chain claims.
    *   Updated PULSE pages to highlight the one-line audit, daily LinkedIn draft, and Market sources.

### Changed
*   **Product Pages:**
    *   Rebuilt the FACE page to document current functionality without demo or mock claims, and updated its tour to match the current app.
    *   Refreshed PULSE pages to align with Release 10 and the shipped product.
    *   Updated general product pages to reflect the current TIDE console, River's desktop, and documentation.
    *   Replaced remaining public mentions of "CORE" with "TIDE."
*   **Homepage:**
    *   Rebuilt the homepage to focus on one product and one first step, targeting field operations and revenue leaders.
    *   Shortened the homepage content.
*   **Site Structure:**
    *   Standardized the footer to a single five-column layout with functional language links.
    *   Removed Android APK downloads from PULSE pages.
    *   Removed an internal source citation from a public industries page.

### Fixed
*   **Privacy:**
    *   Linked the TIDE privacy policy from the general policy page.
    *   Aligned FACE's privacy policy with the app state following `face#1955`.
*   **Site:**
    *   Corrected the public contact address to `contact@runink.org`.
    *   Fixed full-width row labels on mobile devices and added underlines to comparison note links on the homepage.
