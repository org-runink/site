# Everything below is one decision stated twice: crawlers that answer questions
# are welcome, and the things that are not pages are not offered to them.
#
# Why allow the answer engines at all. This site's argument is that a buyer
# should be able to check a claim rather than take it on trust, and the papers
# and product pages are written to be read that way. A model that can quote
# them is doing the thing the pages were written for. A robots.txt is a
# request, not a control; we publish nothing here we would not say to
# somebody's face.
