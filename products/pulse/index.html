The review queue

Nothing goes out until you say so.

Every draft, whether a post, a cold email, a call script or a whitepaper, arrives with a status and an approve and a reject against it. At any moment you can see what is waiting on you.

Where the line between those controls falls →
  • Channels start disarmed. A global switch, narrowed by one for each channel, decides what may publish. A channel that is off sends nothing, whatever status a draft carries.
  • You watch it being written. Research and copy stream onto the screen as they are produced, so a wrong angle is caught in the second paragraph, not on page nine.
  • Your material stays in. Your positioning, your customer lists and your pipeline are processed inside your own network.

From finding out where you stand to keeping who you win

Each stage feeds the next from the same understanding of your business, so nobody writes a fresh brief for every tool.

diagnose

Site Audit

A web address goes in. Back comes a relevance score across nine measures, from search and AI visibility to trust and retention, a business diagnosis, and ranked recommendations you can act on from the same screen.

attract

Market research

The topics moving in your sector, the channels your competitors publish on, and the podcasts, publications and events where your subject belongs. Mark a result useful or not, and the next round learns from it.

attract

Lead prospecting

Describe a niche and PULSE finds matching companies on the public web. For each one it drafts a cold email, a call script and a LinkedIn message, written from what is known about that company.

create

One brief, every channel

A single brief becomes posts shaped for each channel, plus whitepapers, podcasts, short videos, decks, infographics, reports and courses. A 30-day plan says what publishes, where and when.

retain

Follow-up that shows a cooling account

Customer 360 profiles and journey maps show where each account stands. Structured 30-day cycles carry an evolution score, so a quiet relationship shows up as a trend, not a surprise at renewal.

cost

A machine, not a meter

The work runs on machines you own, on ordinary processors. Producing more material does not produce a bigger bill, so the second angle on a campaign gets tried.

The first afternoon moves no data anywhere

  1. 1

    Audit your own site

    PULSE runs on a single workstation. Point Site Audit at your website and read the nine scores, the ranked recommendations and the diagnosis.

  2. 2

    Run one piece end to end

    Take the biggest gap the audit names. Brief a whitepaper on it, watch it stream in, edit it, approve it, and fan it out into posts and emails.

  3. 3

    Then decide

    If the output is good enough for your name to go on it, installing PULSE on your own machines and connecting your accounts is a short next conversation.
