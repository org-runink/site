PULSE — Privacy Policy

This policy covers the PULSE app published by Runink (Android application id org.runink.pulse, and the web app) and the PULSE backend it talks to. For the rest of the Runink platform, see the general privacy policy.

PULSE is a marketing workspace sold to organisations per seat. You use it through your organisation’s PULSE deployment.

Who can use PULSE

You sign in with your Google account. The first sign-in creates your PULSE account on your organisation’s deployment, if your organisation has admitted your address. The app does not sell seats.

What PULSE collects

  • Your account: your name and email address from Google sign-in, and your role. Stored encrypted.
  • What you make in PULSE: audits of the websites you ask about, drafts, campaigns, plans, leads and follow-ups, your business profile and brand colours.
  • Images and files you add: logos, pictures and documents you pick or take with the camera app.
  • Voice commands: when you use the voice bar or the assistant call, your speech is sent to Runink’s own speech-to-text service and turned into text. The audio is not kept; the text of the command is written to the server log.
  • Connected accounts: when you connect LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube or an email mailbox, the access tokens they issue, stored encrypted, and what PULSE reads with them (your posts’ results, comments and messages on those accounts).
  • Usage: how many tasks you ran today, for your seat’s daily limit.

PULSE does not collect your location, contacts, calendar, health data or device identifiers. The app has no advertising or analytics SDK.

Data about other people

PULSE finds business leads from public sources and from your website form: company names, public contact details and the pages they came from. It keeps them for your organisation’s use in PULSE.

How it is used and shared

  • Your data is used to run PULSE for you and your organisation.
  • AI in PULSE runs on Runink’s own servers. No third-party AI service receives your data.
  • PULSE publishes to LinkedIn, Instagram or YouTube, sends an email from your mailbox, or sends a WhatsApp or SMS message only after you approve it, and only when your organisation has turned publishing on.
  • Runink does not sell your data.

Deleting your account

In the app: Account › Privacy and your account › Delete my account. This signs you out everywhere and deletes your account and what PULSE keeps under it. Posts already published stay on the networks they went to.

Without the app: email privacy@runink.org from your account’s address, with the subject “Delete my PULSE account”. What is deleted and what is kept: Delete your account.

Children

PULSE is a marketing workspace for businesses. It is not directed at children, and only people an organisation has admitted can use it.

Changes

We will update this page when PULSE’s behaviour changes, and update the date below.

Your rights and complaints

Runink is published by Technologies Runink Inc., Montréal, Québec, Canada.

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to give you a copy. Email privacy@runink.org. We read that mailbox, and we answer within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec or to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

This policy is governed by the laws of Québec and the federal laws of Canada that apply there.
