{{/* --------------------------------------------------------------------- GROUND PREFERENCE — applied before first paint. WHY IT IS INLINE AND HERE. The stored preference has to be on before the browser paints anything. Loaded as an external script, or placed at the end of , the page paints on the default ground first and then jumps — a white flash on every navigation for anyone who chose dark, which is worse than not offering the choice. So: one small synchronous script, after tokens.css (so the custom properties exist) and before any content. WHAT IT DOES NOT DO. It does not consult prefers-color-scheme. The light sheet is a decision, not a default someone forgot to change: the review of the customer-facing material found the black canvas hard to read and painful to print, and these are pages that get printed and carried into meetings. A visitor whose OS is in dark mode still gets the sheet until they ask for otherwise — and then that choice is remembered. It also never *removes* a ground the template set deliberately. The whitepapers declare data-ground="console" on because that deck is genuinely dark; this only overrides it when the reader has expressed a preference of their own. --------------------------------------------------------------------- */}}
