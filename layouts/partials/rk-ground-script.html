{{/* --------------------------------------------------------------------- GROUND PREFERENCE — applied before first paint. WHY IT IS INLINE AND HERE. The stored preference has to be on before the browser paints anything. Loaded as an external script, or placed at the end of , the page paints on the default ground first and then jumps — a white flash on every navigation for anyone who chose dark, which is worse than not offering the choice. So: one small synchronous script, after tokens.css (so the custom properties exist) and before any content. WHAT IT DOES NOT DO. It does not consult prefers-color-scheme. The ground is a decision rather than a default someone forgot to change — it is the console as of 2026-09-14, and tokens.css 3b records why it moved there from the sheet. A visitor whose OS is in light mode still gets the console until they ask for otherwise, and then that choice is remembered. The printed sheet is unaffected either way: tokens.css binds both grounds to white and black under @media print, which is what let the ground move at all without reopening the argument about pages being carried into meetings. It also never *removes* a ground the template set deliberately. The whitepapers declare data-ground="console" on because that deck is genuinely dark; this only overrides it when the reader has expressed a preference of their own. --------------------------------------------------------------------- */}}
