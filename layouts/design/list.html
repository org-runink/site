Draft — visual identity

Where a claim stands

Runink publishes where every claim stands instead of publishing a number. That habit is the identity. One shape carries it, and the shape works in greyscale, at eleven pixels, and on a photocopy.

Runs

Solid: a record. Something that exists and was observed. Also first-party.

Hypothetical

Hollow: a description. Something stated rather than measured. Also drawn, self-declared, not measured.

The rule about colour

Colour has two jobs

Signal — one orange, and it means a person can act here. Links, focus, the primary action, the approval seam. It is never used for emphasis or decoration, so an orange thing on a Runink page is always something you can do.

Category — which industry or product this is. A category colour is never legible on its own; the industry is always named in text beside it, which is why two of the five can sit close in hue without costing anything.

Nothing else. Standing is fill. Emphasis is weight. Hierarchy is scale and rule. In particular, standing is deliberately not colour: hue would make runs look good and hypothetical look bad, and they are not better and worse — they are different kinds of statement.

The mark

The drawing holds. The file did not.

A herding dog in profile: it reads a field, gathers what is scattered into one picture, and brings it to a named handler who decides. Nothing about that needs redrawing. What needed fixing was the artefact — a 512px raster with a cream background baked in, which is why the site header has always had to crop it into a tile. It is now one vector path set to currentColor, so it takes the ink of whatever ground it sits on, at any size, on paper.
