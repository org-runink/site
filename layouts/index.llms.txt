## True of FACE, TIDE and PULSE

Each is sold on its own subscription, priced by the person or by the machine
it runs on, never as a bundle. Every drafted action waits for a named person;
approving it is what sends it, and who approved it is recorded. Each one can
run on Runink's shared machines, in the customer's own cloud account, or on the
customer's own premises.

We have not been audited against SOC 2, ISO 27001 or any other framework by an
external auditor. The software is built against them and will tell you what it
checked; it does not claim a certification it does not hold. There are no
published customer results, case studies or return-on-investment figures on
this site, and that is deliberate — where a number would go, the pages name the
measure and say where to find it in your own systems instead.
