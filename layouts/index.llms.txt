# Runink

> {{ .Site.Params.description | default "Runink FACE reads the logistics records a business already holds, compares each one against the rule that governs it, and puts a drafted action in front of the person who owns the decision. It runs on infrastructure the customer controls." }}

Runink builds three things. **Runink FACE** is the product: it reads records a
logistics or finance operation already holds, compares each against the rule that
governs it, and drafts an action for a named person to approve. **Runink PULSE**
is a separate product, for market analysis and marketing. **Runink CORE** is not a
product — it is the platform both run on, which is the answer to where data is
processed and who can see it.

Two things are true of all of it and are worth knowing before reading further.
Every drafted action waits for a named person; approving it is what sends it, and
who approved it is recorded. And the models run on hardware the customer
controls — nothing is sent to an outside model provider.

We have not been audited against SOC 2, ISO 27001 or any other framework by an
external auditor. The software is built against them and will tell you what it
checked; it does not claim a certification it does not hold. There are no
published customer results, case studies or return-on-investment figures on this
site, and that is deliberate — where a number would go, the pages name the
measure and say where to find it in your own systems instead.
