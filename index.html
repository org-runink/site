Questions

Recovery

What is revenue leakage in logistics?

Money a shipper was owed, or should never have paid, and did not get back: carrier overcharges, bills paid twice, claims left to expire, demurrage on a held container and orders that miss their window.

How do I find freight overcharges without checking every invoice by hand?

Compare each invoice with the records behind it: the receipt, the weight and the rate that applied that day. Runink FACE does that for every invoice and drafts the dispute for your analyst to approve.

How do I stop freight claims from expiring?

Make each claim cheap to build. FACE gathers the freight bill, the proof of delivery and your service terms, and drafts the claim inside the carrier’s filing window.

How it works

What is an actionable twin?

A live copy of a site, an asset or an order, kept current from your own records. FACE checks each twin against your rules and turns what differs into a drafted action for a person to approve.

How does FACE find the root cause of a loss?

For each claim it lists the causes and estimates how much fixing each one would change the outcome, then ranks them, with the records behind each.

Do we need to replace our TMS, WMS or ERP?

No. FACE reads from the systems you already run and leaves your records as they are.

Buying

Is FACE an outsourced recovery service?

No. It is software your team runs, and a named person approves every dispute, claim and order. Prices, including the success fee on recovered claims, are on the pricing page.

Does our data leave our servers?

On the Dedicated and Enterprise licences FACE runs on your own servers or cloud account. On every licence, nothing is sent to an outside AI model provider.

Who in the company uses it?

Field operations managers for fulfilment orders and exceptions, claims and freight audit teams for disputes and claims, and revenue leaders for what was recovered and why it was lost.
