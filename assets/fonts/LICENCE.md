# Fonts bundled with the whitepapers section

These faces are self-hosted deliberately. The whitepapers argue that a company's
material should be processed on hardware it controls; a request to a third-party
font host while serving that argument would contradict it. Nothing on these pages
is fetched from another origin.

## How these were produced

Subset to Latin, punctuation, currency, sub/superscripts and arrows, then converted
to WOFF2. Full family (900 KB) reduced to 188 KB across six faces.
