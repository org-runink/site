problems_heading: "Where it goes wrong"
problems:
  - title: "A break that grows inside the range you always clear"
    body: "Each month's movement sits within what has been cleared before, so no single month escalates. What is unusual is the three-month sequence, and nobody is reading the sequence."
  - title: "The verification rule on a payment destination"
    body: "A change to where money goes is governed by a rule. That rule is enforced in the system, performed by a person following a procedure, or neither — and which one it is has a habit of surfacing after the event rather than before it."
  - title: "Contracts nobody reads again after signature"
    body: "Supplier agreements carry obligations on service levels, sub-contracting, data handling and notification. Reading the contract against the observed relationship is a rules-against-records comparison at a volume nobody has staffed."
  - title: "Rate cards applied across a portfolio"
    body: "Tiers, thresholds and product terms are a reconciliation between what the terms say and what was actually charged. It gets done periodically, for the same reason as everything else here: volume."

outcomes_heading: "What changes"
outcomes:
  - "Every finding carries its reasoning, the rule it invoked, the records it cited and a proposed action — and is then graded by a second party that arrives through a different door on different credentials, so nothing grades its own work."
  - "Where a finding claims a rate, the raw counts behind it travel with it and the arithmetic is recomputed. Two readings of the same quantity that disagree are recorded as unable to judge, never averaged into a third figure neither party observed."
  - "The record covers what was allowed, what was refused and what failed — the time, the person by verified identity, the action and the outcome — with each entry fingerprinted against the one before it."
  - "Anything that cannot be undone waits for a named person. Where no person is reachable, the gate can be set to refuse rather than to proceed."
