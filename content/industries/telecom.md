problems_heading: "Where it goes wrong"
problems:
  - title: "A rating change that is right for the promotion and wrong for a legacy plan"
    body: "The affected population is small. It moves no monthly aggregate, it fires no threshold, and it is unlikely to turn up in a sample of a few hundred accounts."
  - title: "Two parties, one stream of traffic"
    body: "Interconnect traffic as you recorded it, against traffic as the counterparty recorded it, against the agreement that sets the rates. Three sources, and one difference that matters."
  - title: "A sample gives you a rate, not a name"
    body: "A monthly sweep against a few hundred accounts produces an estimated error rate. What operations actually needs to fix anything is the affected accounts, individually."
  - title: "Rollout across many small sites"
    body: "Site builds, equipment purchases and rollout programmes carry the same plan, approve, procure, spend, record sequence as any capital programme — spread across many small sites, which makes the sampling problem worse rather than better."

outcomes_heading: "What changes"
outcomes:
  - "Comparisons run at the transaction, so the population tested is the population and the exceptions come back as named accounts rather than as an estimated rate."
  - "Each item arrives with the observation, the rule it invoked, the records it cited, a severity and a specific proposed action. An item that cannot be acted on without a judgement call says so."
  - "The queue is sorted twice — once by impact and exposure, once by how confident an independent assessment is — so the first hour of the day goes to what is both material and clear."
  - "That assessment is allowed to decline. Unable to judge is recorded as unable to judge, and is never rendered as agreement."
