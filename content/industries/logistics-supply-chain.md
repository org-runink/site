problems:
  - title: "The hold nobody joined to the cost"

  - title: "Claims that expire quietly"
    body: "One freight claim means finding the carrier's receipt, matching it to the weighbridge reading, finding the rate that applied on the date, checking the filing deadline and drafting the letter. That is most of a morning — so only the largest ever get filed."
  - title: "Temperature you read after the write-off"
    body: "A reefer drifts out of range overnight and the reading is sitting in the sensor data. The window between saving the load and writing it off is measured in hours, and nobody reads sensor data at two in the morning."
  - title: "Demand that grew on the way up the chain"
    body: "Every stage rounds up to a full case and adds a margin of safety, so the plant builds for demand that never existed. The amplification lives in the sequence, and the sequence sits in four systems owned by four different functions."
