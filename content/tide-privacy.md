This policy covers the **Runink TIDE** console, on the web and as the Android
app published by Runink (application id `org.runink.tide`), and the TIDE
deployment it connects to. For the rest of the Runink platform, see the
[general privacy policy](/privacy).

TIDE is a console for a deployment that you or your organisation run. The app
is a window onto that deployment. We do not host your deployment, and we do
not receive your data through the app.

---

## Who can use TIDE

TIDE is not open to the public. An administrator of your organisation's
deployment adds you. The app has no sign-up form.

---

## What TIDE handles

### Your account

* **Sign-in.** You enter the address of your deployment, then your user name
  and password. If your deployment offers Google sign-in, you can use that
  instead. The app sends only Google's signed identity token to your
  deployment, which checks it. We never see your Google password.
* **Your account record.** Your deployment holds your email address, your
  role and your seat. The app shows your email address.
* **Your session.** Your deployment sets it, and it ends after 12 hours by
  default. Signing out ends it.

### What you do in TIDE

The questions you ask, the chat text you type, the commands you run and the
notes you save go to your deployment and are kept there. On the web, you can
also choose a file to import (for example, a CapEx file). The file goes to
your deployment.

### On your device

The console keeps a few interface choices: your language, light or dark theme,
the last instance you chose, and whether the side rail is collapsed. On the
web, these live in your browser's local storage. Clear your browser data, or
the app's data on Android, to remove them.

### Microphone

The web console can use the microphone for voice commands, and only when you
press the voice control. The recording goes to your deployment, which turns it
into text. **The Android app does not ask for the microphone.**

---

## What TIDE does **not** collect

* **No advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service** in the app.
  It contains no Firebase, Crashlytics, Sentry or similar library.
* **No location, contacts, calendar, messages, photos or device identifiers.**
  The Android app asks for one permission, internet access.
* **No backup of app data.** Android backup and device transfer are switched
  off for the app.

---

## Where your data goes

**The app talks to one server: the deployment whose address you entered.** It
connects over an encrypted connection to the instance you enter. We do not
receive what you send. The owner of your deployment is
responsible for that data under their own policy, and the
[general policy](/privacy) covers what Runink handles for your organisation's
account.

The one outside party the app can contact is **Google**, for sign-in, and only
when you choose Google sign-in. Google's privacy policy applies to that step.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

---

## Deleting your data

* **On your device:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage.
* **On your deployment:** ask your administrator to delete your account or the
  data it holds.
* **Anything Runink can do:** write to the contact below.

## Children

TIDE is a business console for administrators. It is not meant for children.

Runink is published by **Technologies Runink Inc.**, Montréal, Québec, Canada.
