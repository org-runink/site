<h2 class="text-3xl md:text-5xl font-bold text-white tracking-tight">
What PULSE is, and what it is not.
</h2>

<p class="text-xl text-ink-2 leading-relaxed">
Marketing today is fragmented. A site audit tool here, a CRM there, a content generator in a third tab, a scheduler in a fourth. Each one works in isolation. Data flows out to someone's servers for every prompt. The work gets briefed, completed, reviewed, then briefed again from nothing for the next channel. By the time a campaign launches, the positioning that made it clear is already a week old.
</p>

<p class="text-xl text-ink-2 leading-relaxed">
PULSE is the cockpit that owns that whole funnel: one app, one shared understanding of your brand, one place where research feeds creation feeds deployment. Every channel is aware of every other. The model is yours — running on your infrastructure, so positioning stays confidential. FACE reads the orders your operation wrote down. PULSE reads the market. CORE is the platform both run on.
</p>
