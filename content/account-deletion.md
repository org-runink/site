This page explains how to ask for your account to be deleted in each app
Runink publishes. None of these apps can delete an account by itself today, so
deletion is done on request.

---

## FACE (`org.runink.face`)

FACE is a business console. Your organisation's administrator adds you, and
can remove you.

**To ask for deletion:** email **privacy@runink.org** from the address you sign
in to FACE with, with the subject "Delete my FACE account". Your organisation's
FACE administrator can also remove you on the instance's Admin page.

**What is deleted:**

* your FACE account record: your email address, password hash, two-factor
  secret, role and creation date;
* your open sessions, which end at once.

**What is kept, and why:**

* Data that belongs to **your organisation** rather than to you personally:
  its fetch runs, run history, decisions, connections and grading results.
  These are deleted at your organisation's request, not at an individual's.
* Server sign-in logs, which record the email address of each sign-in. They
  have no stated retention today.

The [FACE privacy policy](/face-privacy/) describes everything FACE stores.

---

## Luna (`org.runink.luna`)

**To ask for deletion:** email **privacy@runink.org** from the Google account you
sign in to Luna with, with the subject "Delete my Luna account". What Luna
stores, and what deletion removes, is described in the
[Luna privacy policy](/luna-privacy/).
